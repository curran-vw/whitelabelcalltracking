Frequently asked questions

Common questions about white label call tracking for agencies: what it is, how branding and client sub-accounts work, reseller margin, and how to choose or switch platforms.

How white label call tracking works

What is white label call tracking?

It is call tracking software an agency runs but presents as its own. The client sees your logo, your colors, your login domain, and reports with your name. The underlying vendor stays invisible, so you own the client relationship and bill for a branded service.

What does a white-label add-on usually cost?

It varies. Some platforms include light branding on higher tiers, some charge a paid add-on, and some price it as a percentage. CallScaler charges a flat $49 per month, which is kinder to margin than a percentage because the fee does not climb as you add clients.

How do client sub-accounts work?

Each client gets a separate workspace with its own numbers, routing, and reporting, managed from one parent login. The best platforms offer unlimited sub-accounts at a flat tier price, so your cost stays predictable as your roster grows. A per-sub-account charge quietly taxes growth.

Why do per-number costs matter so much for resellers?

Reselling is a spread business. You buy numbers at the platform rate and invoice the client at yours, and the difference is your margin. Per-number costs look small per unit but compound across a full roster. A $0.50 rate against a roughly $3 standard is a large difference at agency scale.

Choosing and switching

Do my clients need to know which platform I use?

No. That is the point of white labeling. With full branding, the client sees only your brand. You should still be honest if asked about partner or affiliate relationships, but the platform itself can stay behind your brand.

Is white label call tracking compliant for calls?

The major platforms support call recording and consent flows, but compliance is your responsibility and varies by vertical and state. Review the FCC guidance on calls and your own legal counsel before running regulated verticals.

How long does it take to switch platforms?

For an agency, plan a few days to recreate branded settings, re-provision client numbers, and run parallel before cutting over. CallScaler's free Pay As You Go tier makes it easy to test a branded setup in parallel before you move clients.

Can I start without a contract?

Yes, on CallScaler. The Pay As You Go tier is $0 per month with no card and no contract, so you can test the product before committing to the Agency tier and the white-label add-on. The established platforms more often involve a demo and a longer commitment.
